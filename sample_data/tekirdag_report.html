Method

  • Reference evapotranspiration ETo: FAO-56 Penman-Monteith, daily step (Eq. 6), soil heat flux G = 0. Actual vapour pressure from dewpoint (Eq. 14), from mean relative humidity (Eq. 19) when dewpoint is missing. Net radiation from measured solar radiation with Rso from Eq. 37 and albedo 0.23. Wind speed at 2 m as provided by NASA POWER (WS2M).
  • Elevation for atmospheric pressure and Rso: elevation of the NASA POWER grid cell.
  • Crop evapotranspiration ETc = Kc x ETo (single crop coefficient). Daily Kc from Kc ini, Kc mid, Kc end with linear change in the development and late season stages (Eq. 66).
  • Kc values: FAO-56 Table 12, checked against the printed publication. Ranges explained by a footnote are separate crop variants; other ranges use their midpoint.
  • Stage lengths: from stage length fields when given, otherwise an FAO-56 Table 11 example chosen by region keyword, then by planting month.
  • Effective rainfall Pe = 0.80 x P (fixed fraction).
  • Net irrigation requirement per dekad (10-day periods, as in CROPWAT): NIR = max(0, sum ETc - sum Pe) for each dekad. Periods with missing climate data get no value; missing days are never filled.
  • Volumes from ellipsoidal field area: 1 mm = 1 m3/da = 10 m3/ha.

Limitations

  • Climate data come from NASA POWER, a global grid of roughly 0.5 degrees. Near coasts and in complex terrain it can differ markedly from local conditions; compare with station data.
  • No soil water storage is modelled: rainfall can only offset demand within the same period (dekad or day). Requirements of crops grown in the rainy season (e.g. winter cereals) are therefore overestimated. A soil water balance is planned for a later version.
  • Kc values are the FAO-56 Table 12 values for a sub-humid climate (RHmin about 45 %, u2 about 2 m/s); the climatic adjustment of Kc mid and Kc end (FAO-56 Eq. 62 and 65) is not applied.
  • FAO-56 Table 11 stage lengths are examples for specific regions and planting dates; local stage lengths give more reliable results.
